WORKFLOW

We ensure a transparent and streamlined process for every project. Our structured workflow allows us to deliver high-end results while strictly meeting every deadline.

QUESTIONS

▼ What do we require from the client before beginning work?

To begin, we typically require architectural drawings (CAD/PDF), 3D models (if available), and a mood board or material specifications. The more complete the initial data, the more accurately we can bring your vision to life.

▼ Is it possible to add something to the workflow?

Our goal is your complete satisfaction. We discuss and coordinate any necessary adjustments in advance to maintain clear communication and ensure the project stays on track.

▼ What is included in the cost of our work?

Our standard deliverables include high-resolution renders (up to 5000px on the long side) and post-processing. Please note that custom 3D modeling of complex assets, stock site purchases, and source file transfers are quoted separately.

▼ What affects the cost?

Several factors can influence the cost of the entire project. The most common ones are:

- the complexity of the entire project;

- the deadlines for its completion; - the scale of the entire project;

- the level of detail; - the season in your project;

- the number and size of the final images.

These are all basic points. The best option would be to send us a request for a quote, and we will calculate everything according to your requirements and your technical specifications.

▼ How is payment made? Do you work on an advance payment basis?

We typically require a 50% upfront payment to initiate the project. The remaining balance is due upon approval of the final previews. High-resolution files without watermarks are delivered immediately following the final payment confirmation.

▼ What does project freezing involve?

If a project is paused for more than 14 days due to a lack of feedback, it will be considered 'frozen.' To resume or close the stage, we invoice based on the current progress: 50% for the 'clay render' stage and 100% if color previews have already been delivered.

▼ What changes in the project can lead to an increase in the cost of work?

Let's just say, we always strive to fit within the client's budget and try to avoid unforeseen costs. But there are cases that are not obvious at the initial stage and can lead to very significant changes in the final project.These can include:

- changes in the camera angle;

- additional angles;

- changes in design or its variations;

- changes in the season;

- changes in time (day or night).

▼ How to communicate with us?

To ensure precision and avoid any misunderstandings, we accept all feedback in written form or via Miro comments. For file sharing, we use Dropbox to keep your project assets organized and secure.
